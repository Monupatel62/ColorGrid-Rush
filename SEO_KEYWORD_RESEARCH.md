# ColorGrid Rush SEO Keyword Research

Date: 2026-09-16

## Research method and limits

- Google Trends was checked for `match-3 game`, `color matching game`, and `puzzle game` using Worldwide / Past 12 months. It confirms these are broad topic clusters, but this environment did not provide a reliable numeric export.
- Google Search result requests were rate-limited with HTTP 429, so no competitor rankings, backlink counts, or search-volume numbers are claimed here.
- Google Keyword Planner requires an account and campaign access. Search volume and CPC should be added after the owner exports data for the target country and language.
- No invented volume, difficulty, ratings, reviews, or backlink claims are included.
